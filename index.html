The last mile

Four places care gets lost, and what I do about each.

One question, four angles. Everything I publish, run, or advise on fits in one of these rows.

The referral

Clinical trial access

Fewer than 1 in 20 community oncology patients enter a trial, and the physician and practice they see explain more of that than the patient does. I lead UT Southwestern's prostate cancer trial access program at Parkland Health and community sites (American Cancer Society IMPACT award, PI), direct GU clinical trial optimization at Parkland, and study physician-level referral behavior nationally under an ASCO Career Development Award. Navigation and physician engagement doubled enrollment of underrepresented patients.

National cohort: 205,743 patients, 1,664 physicians, 107 practices. 20% of physicians account for 80% of enrollments. Manuscripts under review.Empowering oncologists · Nat Rev Clin Oncol 2025

The support

Supportive care · MASCC

Supportive care fails quietly, one missed ride or denied prescription at a time. As Co-Chair of MASCC's Health Access and Disparities Committee (member since 2023, co-chair since 2025), I work on who gets palliative care, symptom control, and the practical help that keeps people in treatment: transportation barriers and mortality, financial hardship, the value of early palliative care, and prior-authorization barriers to supportive medications.

Cancer survivors who delay care for lack of transportation have roughly twice the mortality risk.Transportation and mortality · JNCI 2023Value of palliative care · Ann Intern Med 2024

The benefit design

Insurance & care delivery

Which plan a patient holds shapes which oncologists are in reach, which drugs need prior authorization, and how fast treatment starts. I link Medicare Advantage benefit files, formularies, claims, and real-world cohorts to make those connections measurable, so that benefit design can be discussed alongside stage and biomarker status rather than after the fact.

Zero-premium plans, oncologist networks, primary care cost sharing, and treatment initiation under Medicare Advantage.Treatment initiation, MA vs traditional Medicare · JCO OP 2026Oncologist networks under MA · JAMA Netw Open 2026

The workflow

Clinical operations & AI

A guideline is not a treatment until a workflow carries it there. I lead an NCCN-supported initiative at Parkland that embeds reflex biomarker testing and a molecular-actionability dashboard in the EHR for metastatic prostate cancer, serve on the Simmons Cancer Center AI Steering Committee, and evaluate clinical AI where it belongs: inside a live clinic, with enrollment, time to treatment, and equity as the endpoints.

LLM-generated plain-language trial summaries co-designed with patients; scoping review of LLMs across the trial lifecycle and two commentaries on clinical AI governance under review.Real-world pharmaco-epidemiology · JAMA Netw Open 2026
